Cowdoku & friends

Three little logic puzzles. Same rules, different animal.

Trixdoku50 levels with Trixie, and a shared leaderboard. CocodokuThe same 50 levels, with Coco. CowdokuFree play on hand-painted fields.

How to play. Put one animal in every coloured field, one in every row and one in every column. No two may touch, not even corner to corner. Tap a square once to rule it out, twice to place your animal.

Best times and records are kept for everyone who plays.
